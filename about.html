About Awara Hostels & Dormitory

Awara Hostels & Dormitory offers dormitory accommodation in Chowk, Old Lucknow, about 300 metres from the KGMU gate. The stay is intended for medical students, patient attendants and travellers.

Listed facilities include fresh linen, Wi-Fi, 24-hour hot water, CCTV, lockers, kitchen and fridge, washing machine, maintained bathrooms, power backup and RO drinking water. Contact Awara to confirm current facilities, rates and availability for your dates.
