Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Awara Hostels & Dormitory operates this website at awarahostels.com. For privacy questions, contact awarahostel2019@email.com.

Google Business Profile information

With the business owner’s authorization, this website accesses public reviews for Awara Hostels & Dormitory through the Google Business Profile API. Review names, ratings, comments, dates and owner replies may be shown on this website. The API is also used to post a courteous reply to reviews that do not already have an owner response.

Google OAuth credentials are kept in encrypted Vercel project environment variables and are not included in the website source code. The site does not keep a separate database of review content; review responses may be cached by the website delivery network for up to 15 minutes. Google account access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings.

Website bookings, payments and analytics

The website uses Vercel hosting and Vercel Analytics to deliver pages and understand aggregate website visits. If you submit a website booking, Awara stores your name, email address, phone number, accommodation, guest count, dates, optional note, booking status and payment references in a hosted booking database so it can check inventory, manage your booking, send or verify a payment request and record payment confirmation. Awara staff may also record reservations received offline or through OTAs in the same booking calendar. Booking details are retained as needed to manage the reservation and related business records.

For international website bookings, the site may send the booking reference and USD amount to PayPal to create and verify checkout. For domestic bookings, the site may send your name, email, phone, booking reference and INR amount to PayU to provide hosted payment options such as UPI when enabled on Awara’s merchant account. PayPal and PayU process payment details; this website does not collect or store card details. PayPal.Me may be used as a manual fallback, and those payments are not automatically verified by the website.

Sharing and contact

Awara does not sell or rent personal information. Hosting and delivery providers process information as needed to provide the website. Contact Awara to ask about information handled through a booking request.
