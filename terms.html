Booking and stay information

Booking requests

Sending a request through this website does not reserve a bed or confirm a booking. Awara must confirm availability before your booking is final. The 2BHK is available only when both 1BHK rooms are vacant and requires prior confirmation.

Published tariffs

Hall Dormitory ₹299 per bed/night; 4-Bed Dormitory ₹350 per bed/night; 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory ₹399 per bed/night; 1BHK Cooler Room ₹999 per room/night for up to 2 guests; 1BHK AC Room ₹1,500 per room/night for up to 3 guests; 2BHK Entire Room ₹2,999 per night for up to 5 guests or ₹3,500 per night for 6–8 guests (maximum 8), with prior confirmation required. Confirm availability and any applicable taxes with Awara.

Payment and cancellation

For international bookings, Awara may send a PayPal Checkout link for a specific USD amount only after confirming availability and agreeing the final amount with the guest. PayPal Checkout verifies the payment status, but a paid amount does not itself reserve or confirm a stay; Awara confirms the booking separately. If PayPal Checkout is unavailable, Awara may share the external PayPal.Me link and the guest must enter only the amount quoted by Awara. PayPal cannot receive domestic payments from Indian customers. Contact Awara for domestic payment options. Confirm any advance amount, applicable taxes, cancellation and refund terms before agreeing to a booking.

Check-in and identification

Check-in and check-out times should be confirmed with Awara before arrival. Guests should carry a valid photo ID. Please contact Awara to receive the complete house rules before booking.
