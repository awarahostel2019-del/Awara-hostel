Near Chowk and KGMU

Stay in Old Lucknow, about 300 metres from the KGMU gate and close to Chowk's local food and heritage sights.

Explore Chowk, Lucknow

Awara Hostels is in the historic Chowk area, with local food spots such as Tunday Kababi, Mubeen Restaurant and Idris Biryani nearby. Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara and the lanes of Old Lucknow are also within the wider Chowk area. Check a map for the route and distance that best fits your visit.

For guests visiting KGMU, the hostel's location about 300 metres from the gate can make it easier to stay close to the hospital. Medical students, patient attendants and travellers can choose between dormitory beds and private rooms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the starting price for a dormitory bed?

The listed rates start at ₹299 per bed per night in the Hall Dormitory, ₹350 in the 4-Bed Female Dormitory and ₹399 in the 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory.

Which dorm has AC?

Only the 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory has split AC. The hall dormitory has a fan, and the 4-Bed Female Dormitory has a cooler.

Is there a washing machine and a kitchen?

Yes. Guests can ask the host about washing machine use. The common kitchen has a refrigerator, electric kettle, gas stove and cooking utensils.

How do I make a booking?

Choose dates and accommodation on the official booking page to see the booking options. A booking is confirmed only after the required payment is received and verified. For questions, message Awara on WhatsApp.
