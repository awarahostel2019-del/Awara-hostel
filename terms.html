Booking requests

Sending a request through this website does not reserve a bed or confirm a booking. Awara must confirm availability before your booking is final. Prior confirmation is required for 2BHK bookings.

Published tariffs

Hall Dormitory ₹299 per bed/night; 4-Bed Dormitory ₹350 per bed/night; 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory ₹399 per bed/night; 1BHK Cooler Room ₹999 per room/night; 1BHK AC Room ₹1,500 per room/night; 2BHK Entire Room ₹2,999 per room/night, with prior confirmation required. Confirm availability and any applicable taxes with Awara.

Payment and cancellation

This website does not take online payment. Ask Awara to confirm the payment method, any advance amount, cancellation and refund terms before you agree to a booking. Those details are not specified on this website.

Check-in and identification

Check-in and check-out times should be confirmed with Awara before arrival. Guests should carry a valid photo ID. Please contact Awara to receive the complete house rules before booking.
