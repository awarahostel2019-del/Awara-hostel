# Awara Hostels & Dormitory

Awara Hostels & Dormitory offers dormitory accommodation in Chowk, Old Lucknow, about 300 metres from KGMU gate. It is intended for medical students, patient attendants and travellers.

## Facilities listed on the website

- Fresh linen and dormitory bunks
- Free Wi-Fi and 24x7 hot water
- CCTV and secure lockers
- Kitchen and fridge
- Washing machine, maintained bathrooms, power backup and RO drinking water
