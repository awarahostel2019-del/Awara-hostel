Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Awara Hostels & Dormitory operates this website at awarahostels.com. For privacy questions, contact awarahostel2019@email.com.

Google Business Profile information

With the business owner’s authorization, this website accesses public reviews for Awara Hostels & Dormitory through the Google Business Profile API. Review names, ratings, comments, dates and owner replies may be shown on this website. The API is also used to post a courteous reply to reviews that do not already have an owner response.

Google OAuth credentials are kept in encrypted Vercel project environment variables and are not included in the website source code. The site does not keep a separate database of review content; review responses may be cached by the website delivery network for up to 15 minutes. Google account access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings.

Website requests, payments and analytics

The website uses Vercel hosting and Vercel Analytics to deliver pages and understand aggregate website visits. Booking request details are sent only when a visitor chooses to send them by WhatsApp or email. For an international booking, Awara may create a signed, expiring PayPal Checkout quote after confirming availability and the amount with the guest. The website sends the quote reference and amount to PayPal to create and verify checkout; PayPal processes the payment details. Awara’s website does not collect or store card details and does not keep a separate payment database. PayPal.Me may be used as a manual fallback; those payments are not automatically verified by the website.

Sharing and contact

Awara does not sell or rent personal information. Hosting and delivery providers process information as needed to provide the website. Contact Awara to ask about information handled through a booking request.
