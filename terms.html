Booking and stay information

Website bookings

International bookings for dorm beds and 1BHK rooms check current website inventory and place a short hold while the guest pays the full stay amount. The website confirms these bookings after PayPal verifies full payment. If payment is not completed before the hold expires, the hold ends and availability may change.

Domestic booking requests and all 2BHK requests wait for staff action. Staff checks current availability; 2BHK is available only when both 1BHK rooms are vacant for every night. Domestic bookings are confirmed after staff verifies manual payment. Website inventory reflects the availability Awara manages here; staff records offline and OTA bookings in the same calendar. OTA calendars do not sync automatically.

Half of dorm bed capacity is offered online by default to leave room for offline guests. Staff can adjust the saleable inventory by date in the booking desk.

Published tariffs

Hall Dormitory ₹299 per bed/night; 4-Bed Dormitory ₹350 per bed/night; 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory ₹399 per bed/night; 1BHK Cooler Room ₹999 per room/night for up to 2 guests; 1BHK AC Room ₹1,500 per room/night for up to 3 guests; 2BHK Entire Room ₹2,999 per night for up to 5 guests or ₹3,500 per night for 6–8 guests (maximum 8), when both 1BHK rooms are vacant for the full stay. The amount shown at checkout is calculated from these tariffs and the selected stay dates. Ask Awara about any applicable taxes before paying.

Payment, cancellation and refunds

International online payments are processed through PayPal Checkout in USD using a current reference exchange rate. PayPal or a card provider may apply its own conversion rate or fees. Domestic online checkout is not connected yet. Awara does not store card details on this website.

For cancellation or refund requests, contact Awara directly using the details on the contact page. A refund is not automatic; Awara will review the request and confirm the outcome with the guest. PayPal.Me, if used as a manual fallback, does not automatically create or confirm a website booking.

Payments and receipts at the property

Awara staff may record advance, partial or full payments made by cash, card, UPI, or an OTA. A receipt records the amount and payment mode entered by staff; guests should check the details before leaving. A final invoice can be issued at checkout. Contact Awara promptly if a receipt detail is incorrect.

Check-in and identification

Check-in and check-out times should be confirmed with Awara before arrival. Guests must present a valid government photo ID, such as Aadhaar, driving licence, voter ID or passport. Staff may record the ID category and whether it was checked; this website does not ask staff to enter the ID number or upload an ID image. Please contact Awara to receive the complete house rules before booking.
