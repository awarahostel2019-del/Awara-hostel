Booking and stay information

Website bookings

When you submit a regular website booking, Awara checks its current website inventory and temporarily holds the requested beds or room while the payment link is active. The website shows the hold expiry. The booking is confirmed after the payment provider verifies full payment and the website records the confirmation. If the payment is not completed before the hold expires, the hold ends and availability may change.

Website inventory reflects the availability Awara manages on this site. Awara staff also records offline and OTA bookings in the staff booking desk. Third-party travel platforms may show separate availability and prices; check the platform or contact Awara if information differs.

2BHK bookings are available online when both 1BHK rooms are vacant for every night of the stay. The site checks both rooms before checkout and temporarily holds the combined inventory while the guest pays. The booking is confirmed automatically after verified payment.

Published tariffs

Hall Dormitory ₹299 per bed/night; 4-Bed Dormitory ₹350 per bed/night; 8-Bed Mixed Dormitory ₹399 per bed/night; 1BHK Cooler Room ₹999 per room/night for up to 2 guests; 1BHK AC Room ₹1,500 per room/night for up to 3 guests; 2BHK Entire Room ₹2,999 per night for up to 5 guests or ₹3,500 per night for 6–8 guests (maximum 8), when both 1BHK rooms are vacant for the full stay. The amount shown at checkout is calculated from these tariffs and the selected stay dates. Ask Awara about any applicable taxes before paying.

Payment, cancellation and refunds

International online payments are processed through PayPal Checkout in USD using a current reference exchange rate. PayPal or a card provider may apply its own conversion rate or fees. Domestic checkout will be shown only when an approved Indian payment provider is connected. Awara does not store card details on this website.

For cancellation or refund requests, contact Awara directly using the details on the contact page. A refund is not automatic; Awara will review the request and confirm the outcome with the guest. PayPal.Me, if used as a manual fallback, does not automatically create or confirm a website booking.

Check-in and identification

Check-in and check-out times should be confirmed with Awara before arrival. Guests should carry a valid photo ID. Please contact Awara to receive the complete house rules before booking.
